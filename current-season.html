How it works

Eight teams qualify after Week 14. Ties in the standings are broken by total PF. Playoffs are Weeks 15–17, with no byes or reseeding.

Predictions use this season’s scoring and the remaining schedule. They do not account for lineup changes or injuries. A high playoff chance is not a guaranteed spot.

Your picks update the selected team’s outlook. The league table always shows the current forecast without picks.

Calculation details

The league forecast uses 50,000 simulations; team scenarios use 20,000. Team scoring is pulled toward the league average using four weeks of league-average scores. Future scores use a shared scoring spread. Picked winners receive simulated scores consistent with that result.

Rooting suggestions compare your playoff chances under each winner. Close calls need at least 300 samples per outcome and an edge above the estimated 95% sampling margin. These comparisons are estimates, not guarantees. Sampling margins do not cover uncertainty in the scoring model.

Guaranteed win totals are checked against the remaining schedule, including tied games and changes in PF. With picks, a quicker check may leave the outcome undecided. Simulation results alone never establish a clinch or elimination. Future game ties are not simulated. Standings use wins plus half a win for ties; exact ties on both record and PF need commissioner resolution.

Luck standings

Actual wins compared with the wins expected from weekly scoring.

Luck standings through the approved current-season week.
#Owner / teamRecordExpected WLuck
About these stats

Expected wins are based on how many teams you outscored each week. Outscoring 8 of the other 11 teams earns 8/11 of an expected win for that week. Add those weekly amounts together to get your season’s expected wins. Luck = actual wins − expected wins. Positive means you’ve won more games than your scoring would suggest; negative means you’ve won fewer.
